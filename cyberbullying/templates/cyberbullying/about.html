About this Platform

This platform helps people affected by harassment, cyberbullying, stalking, threats, impersonation, doxxing, hate speech and blackmail report incidents, track their case, and reach an administrator directly through live chat.

You can report an incident whether or not you create an account. Registered users get a personal dashboard with complaint history and notifications; anonymous reporters get a secure tracking code they can use to check on their case and send follow-up messages without registering.

Every report is reviewed by an administrator, who can request more information, record investigation notes, and move a case through its resolution.

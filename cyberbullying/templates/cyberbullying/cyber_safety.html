Cyber Safety Resources

  • Save evidence (screenshots, URLs, timestamps) before blocking or reporting a bully.
  • Report abusive content to the platform it occurred on first, then to us if it continues.
  • Review and tighten your privacy and account security settings after an incident.
  • Avoid engaging directly with the person who is bullying or threatening you.
  • If you feel unsafe, contact local law enforcement in addition to reporting here.
